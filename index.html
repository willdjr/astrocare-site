AstroCare
Autonomous Space Healthcare

On the way to Mars, the crew is the hospital.

Earth can be 20 minutes away, or completely silent behind the Sun. AstroCare watches each astronaut's health against their own Earth baseline and guides the crew, one step at a time, when something goes wrong.

  • ❤
    Crew monitoring · smart-shirt vitals, dosimeters and blood tests compared with each astronaut's own Earth baseline.
  • ⬡
    Ship sensors · CO₂, O₂, pressure and radiation per module, with NASA-STD-3001 limits and actionable alerts.
  • ✚
    Voice emergency mode · “Hey AstroCare”: offline AI identifies the emergency, the crew confirms, NASA-based protocols guide step by step.
  • ◎
    AI observes, rules decide · every action is logged into an automatic care record for the flight surgeon.
